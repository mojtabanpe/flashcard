# Vocabulary cards

For `word skills advanced`:
- Keep card text entirely in English; do not include Persian meanings or translations.
- Put the word or phrase on the front and its English definition on the back.
- Store English examples in the separate `examples` field. Reveal them using the `!` button on the answer side, initially collapsed for each card.
- Store the lesson in the separate `unit` field, using lowercase short labels such as `u19`. Do not append the lesson title or section to the definition.
- Preserve card IDs and review history when correcting existing cards, and avoid duplicate vocabulary.
